## 1. Protocol

* 28 synthetic students → **train 18 / test 10**; episode = **20 recommendations**; action space = 36 (topic × difficulty).
* Training: 4 tabular/deep agents × **5 seeds** ([0, 1, 2, 3, 4]) × 2000 episodes; early-stopped checkpoint selected on 4 train students (greedy val reward), then the frozen checkpoints are evaluated on the **test split**: 10 students × 3 eval seeds → **30 matched cells** (RL strategies averaged over their 5 training seeds inside each cell).
* 3 baselines (Random, Difficulty-Based, Prerequisite-Based) use the identical readout harness.
* Significance: paired t + Wilcoxon (matched cells), Welch t + Cohen's d (RL agent vs best baseline, independent 30-cell sets).

## 11. Mandated notes

**(a) Why 20-step episodes and 5 seeds (not 50-step / 1 seed or the spec's illustrative "e.g., 50")?**
`EPISODE_LEN=20` is the frozen, tested geometry of this project (env unit tests, audit harness, epsilon schedules, reward clipping, the archived 3-seed run and every locked artifact are defined at 20 steps). The spec's 50 was an *example*, not a requirement; changing the horizon post-lock would have invalidated the lock, the audit traces and all comparisons against the archived run. 5 training seeds were added instead of the original 3 to report honest mean ± sd (chosen over more episodes: 5 seeds × 2 000 episodes directly reduces seed-variance, which the 3-seed run showed to be the dominant error source — e.g. DQN seed 1 collapses while seeds 0/2 do not). Cost on this host: ≈ 4 agents × 5 seeds × 2 000 episodes train in minutes (per-run times in §5); a 50-step horizon would multiply every env step (train + all studies + evaluation) by 2.5× for no comparability benefit.

**(b) PPO**
PPO exists and was trained: `agents/ppo_agent.py` (`training/train_ppo.py`), 3-seed checkpoints `models/PPO_seed{0,1,2}.pt` + `results/training/PPO_seed*.csv` from the earlier 3-seed run. It is **excluded from the main comparison** by project decision (tabular-vs-deep focus; the 5-seed retrain budget covered the 4 core agents). It is listed as Future Work — a 5-seed PPO retrain on the identical protocol.

**(c) No prereq-state ablation**
Agents never observe the prerequisite DAG: the 26-dim state is (12 mastery + 8 behavioural + 6 error-distribution features; table in `dataset.md`). The DAG only shapes (i) the simulator's success model via the synthetic data generator, (ii) the Prerequisite-Based baseline's action choice, and (iii) the diagnostic `prereq_violation_rate`. There is therefore no "prerequisite feature" to delete — the state ablation removes the 6 error-type features instead (documented in §7).

**(d) Sensitivity protocol**
OFAT on **Q-Learning and DQN only**, train split, **seeds 0, 1** × **600 episodes** (34 trainings, 364.7 s), readout on the first 6 train students × 2 eval seeds = 24 cells/config. Deep-agent `lr` and `batch_size` are **held fixed** at their config defaults (1e-3, 64). Every grid contains and marks its config.py default; total runtime recorded. This is a budgeted study (2 seeds, 600 episodes) — indicative, not a full retrain.

## 12. Caveats & limitations (be honest in the report)

1. **RQ1 headline is negative for RL**: best RL (SARSA 0.0142) < Prerequisite-Based (0.0171), paired p = 9.678e-06; RL only clearly beats Random, and even there paired t vs Wilcoxon disagree (0.05681 vs 0.02085). The DAG-informed heuristic remains the strongest skill-gain policy — a legitimate negative result (the reward-shaped objective optimises reward/calibration, not raw skill gain).
2. **DQN instability**: audit verdicts — seed 1 COLLAPSED, seeds 0/2 NARROW (§9); DQN reward 4.22 vs Q-Learning 14.87 (§2); training loss *rises* over training while max_q grows (fig. `11_dqn_loss_curves.png`) — the frozen DQN checkpoints are the weak link of the deep agents.
3. **State ablation helps DQN** (14.31 vs 6.35 reward, p = 8.6e-4, train-split readout): the 6 error-type features appear harmful here — with only 2 000 episodes the extra input dims may be noise. Do not claim the error features help.
4. **Sensitivity factor `buffer_size` is partly inert** at 600 episodes (12 000 transitions < 50 000): 100k and 50k are bit-identical (§6).
5. **Overestimation rule gap is inert at convergence** (argmax agreement 1.000, §8): the DDQN-vs-DQN rule difference cannot manifest in frozen snapshots; the measured gap (+1.35 vs +0.46) is *between the two trained models*.
6. **Split asymmetry**: main comparison = test split; sensitivity/ablation/reward-weight studies = train-split readouts (their model selections never touch the test split).
7. **Statistical scope**: 30 matched cells (10 students × 3 eval seeds) with RL averaged over 5 training seeds; cells share students across seeds (paired design used deliberately). Sensitivity n = 2 seeds is underpowered by design.
8. **Simulation**: students are synthetic (BKT-like mastery + behaviour parameters); conclusions are simulator-internal.
9. **Reproducibility artifact**: comparing floats across CSV round-trips needs `atol=1e-9` (observed max noise 3.6e-15 in the 50-episode repro gate; full-run reproduction matched the archived 3-seed run at max|diff| = 0 before re-seeding to 5).
10. **PPO** has only the old 3-seed checkpoints (note (b)); 3-seed v1 artifacts are archived at `results_archive_3seed/`.
