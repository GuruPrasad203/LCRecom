Key Takeaways

  1. No RL agent beats the best baseline (Prerequisite-Based) on skill gain. SARSA matches the difficulty baseline and beats Random, but the prerequisite topic ordering is the strongest method on the primary metric (skill gain).
  2. RL wins the reward metric, not the learning metric. SARSA/Q-Learning reach ≈15.2 total reward vs 11.4 for the best baseline, mainly through lower time cost and lower frustration.
  3. Deep RL is unstable. After protocol-compliant retrain, DQN still produces one non-positive training readout per 3 seeds and 37/90 held-out episodes with negative total reward. Double DQN recovers the reward (12.06) but not the skill-gain ranking (0.0135).
  4. Reward is not over-fitted. Five plausible re-weightings change nothing significant (all p > 0.28), so the reward shape is not driving the ranking.
